Only large reservoirs are included, so this is not comparable dam-for-dam with the US map.

Data: CRAD, China Reservoir Attribute Database (Liang, Zhang, Obulkasim, Ye, Song et al., 2026; Zenodo, CC BY 4.0), described in a paper still under review in Earth System Science Data. CRAD lists 5,142 large reservoirs, which hold about 87.5% of China's total reservoir storage according to its authors; China has tens of thousands of smaller reservoirs that are not in it. Shown: the 3,087 with a recorded year in mainland China and Hong Kong (744 of their 892 km³ of storage); 2,041 have no year and are not on the map; the 14 reservoirs CRAD lists in Taiwan are left out. The year is CRAD's "year in which the dam was built, commissioning or refurbishment". Storage is each reservoir's total capacity as listed today, so a dam that was later raised counts at today's size from its completion year. Position: the dam when CRAD gives it, otherwise the centre of the reservoir. Reservoirs CRAD marks as removed disappear in that year. Main purpose is CRAD's first-listed use, grouped.
