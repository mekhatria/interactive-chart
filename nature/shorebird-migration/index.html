Data: Lisovski S, Gosbell K, Minton C, Klaassen M (2020), Movebank Data Repository, CC0: red-necked stints (17 birds, tracked 2016 and 2019) and curlew sandpipers (13 birds, 2018 and 2019), all tagged at Western Port Bay, Victoria, Australia; study: Journal of Animal Ecology. Positions come from light-level geolocators (sunrise and sunset times), so each one is uncertain by roughly 100–200 km or more, and there are none while the Arctic sun never sets. Bird photos: Wikimedia Commons, credited on each photo. Each bird's own 12 months are placed on one calendar year so all 30 fly together; the paths are lightly smoothed (over about a day) and drawn as spline curves between the published positions, for the animation. "On the move" means the bird's position changed by more than 60 km that day. · Highcharts.com
